# Privacy Policy

> This is a draft before legal review. It is not the final text and will be reviewed before release.

This app is a journal for picking today's mood and leaving its size as a tap count. This page explains how the app handles personal information.

## Mood records stay on the device

- Moods, tap counts and the short note of a day are stored in one file on the device (`journal.json`) and nowhere else.
- No record is sent to a server. There is no server to send to.
- There is no account, sign-up or login.
- Changing or wiping the device does not carry the records over.

## Notifications

- The evening reminder is scheduled on the device and runs on the device.
- The reminder text is a fixed greeting. It never names a mood or reveals that a record exists, so the lock screen shows nothing about your moods.

## Exporting and deleting records

- Export saves a file through the share sheet, done by you. The saved file is yours to manage.
- Delete all records removes the record file on the device after a confirmation step.

## Mood records as sensitive information

- Mood records can qualify as sensitive information under privacy law. This app never sends mood information off the device in any form, so nothing is collected by others or shared with third parties.

## Notice for when the map arrives

- There is no map feature today. When one is introduced, this policy will be updated and announced first.
- When introduced, locations will be turned into grid cells of about 1km on the device and only the cell number is sent. Cells with fewer than five participants stay hidden from the map. Sharing a mood is used only by people who turn it on themselves.

## About this app

- This app is not a medical device and does not replace care from a doctor.
- When things feel heavy, the suicide prevention line 109 listens.
