# Privacy Policy

> This is a draft before legal review. It is not the final text and will be reviewed before release.

This app is a journal for picking today's mood and leaving its size as a tap count. This page explains how the app handles personal information.

## Mood records stay on the device

- Moods, tap counts and the short note of a day are stored in one file on the device (`journal.json`) and nowhere else.
- How far the cheer phrases have been shown also stays in a file on the device (`cheer_decks.json`): only a position, never the words.
- Settings such as the theme, language, vibration, sound, cheer phrases, screen shake, tilt to pour, the evening reminder, weather display and the map choices live in the system settings store.
- No mood record is sent to a server. There is no server for mood records.
- There is no account, sign-up or login.
- Changing or wiping the device does not carry the records over.

## Operating system backups may include the data

- App data can be saved along with the device backups: automatic backup and device transfer on Android, iCloud device backup and computer backup on iPhone and iPad. This app does not block backups.
- The backups are guarded by your operating system account and screen lock. This app cannot read or send them.
- Backups stay on because they are the way to get records back.

## Notifications

- The evening reminder is scheduled on the device and runs on the device.
- The reminder text is a fixed greeting. It never names a mood or reveals that a record exists, so the lock screen shows nothing about your moods.

## Exporting and deleting records

- Export saves a file through the share sheet, done by you. The saved file is yours to manage.
- Delete all records removes the record file on the device after a confirmation step.

## Mood records as sensitive information

- Mood records can qualify as sensitive information under privacy law. This app never sends mood information off the device in any form, so nothing is collected by others or shared with third parties.

## Weather display and location

- Location and network access are used only to show current weather next to the date on the mood select screen.
- Location permission is requested only while the app is in use. Coordinates are rounded on the device to one decimal place (roughly an 11 km grid) before being sent to MET Norway (`api.met.no`).
- No mood records or device identifiers are included in the request.
- If you deny location permission or turn off "Show weather" in Settings, location is never requested and no network call is made.

## Notice for when the map arrives

- There is no map feature today. Sharing a mood arrives in stage three, together with an account. The pilot of today sends nothing off the device.
- When one is introduced, this policy will be updated and announced first.
- When introduced, locations will be turned into neighbourhood cells of about 5km² on the device and only the cell number is sent. Cells with fewer than five participants stay hidden from the map. Sharing a mood is used only by people who turn it on themselves.
